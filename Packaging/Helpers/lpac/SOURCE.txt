The minimal patch fixes the APDU stdio backend's inverted success checks,
adds a private stdin request for activation/confirmation codes, and makes the
helper report an auditable DJIO-specific version. The helper remains a separate
AGPL process and communicates with DJIO only through newline-delimited JSON.

Rebuild with:
./scripts/build-lpac-helper.sh
